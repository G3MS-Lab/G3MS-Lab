This paper introduces a digital educational game for teaching singly linked list operations — insertion, deletion, and traversal — through interactive gameplay structured around the LADDER instructional model. The game provides animated visualizations of pointer manipulation and node management, helping students build an accurate mental model of dynamic data structures. Assessment data collected from computer science students indicates statistically significant gains in both conceptual knowledge and problem-solving accuracy.
