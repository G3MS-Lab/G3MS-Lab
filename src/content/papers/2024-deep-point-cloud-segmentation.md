We present a transformer-based architecture for semantic segmentation of large-scale outdoor point clouds captured by LiDAR sensors. Our method introduces a hierarchical attention mechanism that efficiently aggregates local geometric features across multiple scales, achieving state-of-the-art performance on the SemanticKITTI benchmark with 73.2% mIoU while maintaining real-time inference speed on a single GPU.
