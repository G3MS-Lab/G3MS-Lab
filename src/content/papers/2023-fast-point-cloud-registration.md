This paper proposes a novel geometric hashing scheme for establishing robust correspondences between partially overlapping point clouds. By encoding local surface geometry into compact hash signatures, our method achieves correspondence search in sub-linear time and enables real-time registration pipelines for autonomous navigation systems. We demonstrate a 4× speedup over ICP-based methods with comparable accuracy on standard benchmarks.
