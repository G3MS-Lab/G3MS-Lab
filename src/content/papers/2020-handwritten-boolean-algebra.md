This paper presents a system for recognizing handwritten Boolean algebra derivation steps and identifying logical errors in student work. Symbols and operators are segmented and classified using image processing techniques, and the derivation sequence is parsed against Boolean algebra rules to locate incorrect transitions. The system is intended to assist instructors in providing rapid, consistent feedback on handwritten logic assignments.
