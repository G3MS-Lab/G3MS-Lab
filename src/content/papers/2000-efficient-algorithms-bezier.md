This paper presents efficient recursive algorithms for the evaluation, subdivision, and degree elevation of Bézier curves. By reorganising the classical de Casteljau algorithm into a form that maximises data reuse, the proposed methods achieve a significant reduction in floating-point operations for curves of moderate-to-high degree. The algorithms are applicable to both rational and non-rational Bézier forms and are directly implementable in real-time computer graphics pipelines.
