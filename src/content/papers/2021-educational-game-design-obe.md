This paper introduces a model canvas and structured design process for creating educational games aligned with Outcome-Based Education (OBE) principles. The canvas maps game mechanics to learning outcomes, assessment criteria, and curriculum competencies, providing instructors with a practical tool for designing games that are both pedagogically sound and engaging. Case studies from computer science and mathematics courses at KMUTT validate the canvas's effectiveness in producing measurable learning gains.
