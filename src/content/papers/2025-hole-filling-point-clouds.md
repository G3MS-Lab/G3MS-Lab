This paper proposes a novel triangulation-based method for filling holes in 3D point clouds by combining Delaunay triangulation with approximation curve fitting. The method identifies hole boundaries, fits Bézier curves to the boundary edges, and generates new points within the hole region to produce a geometrically consistent surface. Experimental results demonstrate improved hole-filling quality compared to existing methods, particularly for complex curved surfaces.
