This paper presents a digital educational game designed to teach the genetic algorithm through interactive simulation. Using the ADDIE (Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation) instructional design model, the game visually demonstrates key processes including selection, crossover, and mutation on progressively challenging optimization problems. User studies conducted with undergraduate students show measurable improvements in algorithm comprehension and sustained engagement compared to traditional lecture-based instruction.
