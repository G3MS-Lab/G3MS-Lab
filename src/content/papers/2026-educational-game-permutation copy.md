This study presents the development and evaluation of a digital educational game designed to enhance students' understanding of permutation concepts in mathematics. The game was developed using the ADDIE instructional design model to ensure systematic alignment between learning objectives and gameplay mechanics. It incorporates intrinsically integrated game-based learning principles to promote logical reasoning and active problem solving. A pretest-posttest experimental design was employed to examine the effectiveness of the game on students' conceptual understanding, engagement, and problem-solving performance. The results indicate that the proposed game-based learning approach leads to improved learning outcomes compared to traditional instructional methods. These findings support the use of educational games developed under the ADDIE framework as an effective approach for teaching abstract mathematical concepts.
