This paper presents an efficient algorithm for approximating Bézier curves by a sequence of circular arcs to a prescribed tolerance. The method recursively subdivides the curve and fits a circular arc to each segment, guaranteeing that the approximation error remains below the specified bound. The resulting arc-spline representation is directly usable by CNC machining systems and pen plotters, which natively support circular arc interpolation. Comparative experiments demonstrate superior accuracy-to-segment-count ratios over linear approximation methods.
