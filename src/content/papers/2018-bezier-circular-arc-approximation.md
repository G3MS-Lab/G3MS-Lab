This conference paper presents an early approach to approximating Bézier curves using circular arc segments, forming the basis for the subsequent journal extension. The method subdivides the Bézier curve at inflection points and curvature extrema to guarantee single-arc fidelity per segment, then fits the best-matching circular arc using the three-point method. Results show accurate approximations for practical curve degrees used in font design and CNC toolpath generation.
