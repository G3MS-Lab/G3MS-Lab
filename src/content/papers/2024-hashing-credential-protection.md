This paper proposes a hashing scheme that protects user credential information against both channel interception (man-in-the-middle attacks) and server-side data leakage. The approach combines challenge-response authentication with salted hashing to ensure that plaintext passwords are never transmitted or stored, while remaining lightweight enough for deployment in resource-constrained environments. Security analysis and performance benchmarks demonstrate the scheme's practical viability for web applications.
