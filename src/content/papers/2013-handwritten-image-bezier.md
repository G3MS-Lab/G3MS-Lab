This paper proposes a method for approximating a digitized handwritten stroke by a single Bézier curve of appropriate degree. Key feature points along the stroke are extracted using curvature analysis, and control points are optimized to minimize the maximum deviation between the curve and the original raster image. The resulting compact parametric representation enables resolution-independent storage and efficient comparison of handwritten characters.
