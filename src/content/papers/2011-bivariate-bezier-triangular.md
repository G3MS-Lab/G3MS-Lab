This paper introduces a new bivariate basis for constructing triangular Bézier patches that reduces evaluation complexity from cubic to quadratic in the patch degree. The basis is derived from a symmetric recursive formulation that naturally generalises the univariate Bernstein basis to the triangular domain. Theoretical proofs of the basis properties — non-negativity, partition of unity, and endpoint interpolation — are provided, along with efficient de Casteljau-style evaluation algorithms.
