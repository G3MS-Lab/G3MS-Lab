This paper proposes an automated grading pipeline for handwritten mathematics homework that combines stroke segmentation, symbol recognition, and structural analysis to evaluate student solutions. The system identifies mathematical symbols and operators, reconstructs expression trees, and compares them against reference solutions to produce partial-credit scores. Evaluation on a dataset of handwritten calculus and algebra assignments demonstrates promising recognition accuracy and grading consistency comparable to human raters.
