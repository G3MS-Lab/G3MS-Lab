This paper presents an educational game designed to teach fundamental calculus concepts — limits, derivatives, and integrals — through interactive puzzles and visual simulations. The game dynamically renders function graphs and geometric interpretations of calculus operations, allowing students to manipulate parameters and observe real-time changes. Preliminary user evaluation indicates increased motivation and a reduction in common conceptual misconceptions among first-year engineering students.
