This paper presents a digital educational game designed to teach fundamental linear algebra concepts using the LADDER (Learn, Apply, Discover, Develop, Evaluate, Review) instructional model. The game provides interactive visualizations of vector operations, matrix transformations, and eigenvalue problems, enabling students to explore abstract algebraic concepts through gameplay. Evaluation results from undergraduate students show significant improvement in conceptual understanding and learning engagement.
