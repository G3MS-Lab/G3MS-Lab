This paper derives fast recursive algorithms for evaluating both uniform and nonuniform Lagrange and Newton interpolation curves in CAGD. The proposed algorithms reduce computational complexity by exploiting the recurrence relations inherent in these bases, achieving evaluation in linear time with respect to the curve degree. The algorithms are validated against standard de Casteljau and Horner approaches, showing comparable or superior performance for moderate-to-high degree curves.
