Dr. Taweechai Nuntawisuttiwong is a lecturer at the Department of Computer Engineering, KMUTT. His research focuses on geometric curve approximation — particularly Bézier curve fitting and circular arc approximation — as well as 3D point cloud hole filling, digital educational game development, and applied cryptography. He has collaborated extensively with Assoc. Prof. Dr. Natasha Dejdumrong and has published in IEEE Information Visualisation, IEEE JCSSE, and Information Technology and Control.
