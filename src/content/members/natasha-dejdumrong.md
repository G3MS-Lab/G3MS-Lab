Assoc. Prof. Dr. Natasha Dejdumrong is a faculty member at the Department of Computer Engineering, KMUTT. Her research spans computer-aided geometric design (CAGD), curve and surface modeling, 3D point cloud processing, handwriting recognition, and digital educational game development. She has published over 70 papers in international journals and conferences including Computers & Mathematics with Applications, Information Technology and Control, IEEE CGIV, and IEEE Information Visualisation.
