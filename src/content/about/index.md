G3MS Lab (Geometric, Game and Graphics Modeling and Simulation Laboratory) is a research laboratory at the Department of Computer Engineering, King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT).

Our research spans the intersection of geometric computing, interactive media, and applied mathematics. The lab is led by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Natasha Dejdumrong and Dr. Taweechai Nuntawisuttiwong, whose combined work covers over 70 publications in international journals and conferences.

**Computer-Aided Geometric Design (CAGD)** is a central focus of the lab. We develop efficient algorithms for Bézier curve and surface modeling — including degree elevation, subdivision, and novel bivariate basis representations for triangular patches — building on foundational work published in *Computers & Mathematics with Applications* and *Computer Aided Geometric Design*.

In **3D point cloud processing**, we address the challenge of reconstructing complete surfaces from sparse or incomplete scan data. Our work on point cloud hole filling uses geometric interpolation techniques to produce smooth, watertight surfaces suitable for engineering and visualization applications.

The lab also has a long-running program in **digital educational game design**, developing game-based learning tools for mathematics and engineering subjects such as calculus. These projects apply instructional design frameworks to create interactive experiences that improve student motivation and conceptual understanding.

Additional research areas include **handwriting recognition** using geometric feature extraction, **curve approximation** via circular arcs and Bézier fitting for efficient shape representation.
